# Task 4 – Dashboard Design

## Objective

Design an interactive business dashboard that helps stakeholders monitor sales performance and make informed decisions.

## Tool Used

* Power BI

## Dataset

Sales / Financial Dataset

## Dashboard Components

### KPI Cards

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Profit Margin %

### Visualizations

1. Sales Trend Analysis
2. Sales by Category
3. Profit by Region
4. Segment-wise Sales Distribution
5. Top 10 Customers

### Interactive Features

* Year Slicer
* Region Slicer
* Category Slicer
* Segment Slicer

## Key Insights

* Technology category generated the highest sales.
* West region produced the highest profit.
* Consumer segment contributed the largest share of sales.
* Sales showed a positive growth trend over time.

## Conclusion

The dashboard provides a clear view of business performance through KPIs, trend analysis, regional performance, and customer insights. Interactive filters allow stakeholders to explore data efficiently and support data-driven decision-making.
